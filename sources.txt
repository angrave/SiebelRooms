Siebel Center room-type classifications — sources & notes
=========================================================
Compiled 2026-11-10. Types live in allrooms.txt (Conference | Class | Research
| Admin | Unknown). This file records WHERE each designation came from and how
much to trust it.

PRIMARY SOURCES
---------------
[1] CS Faculty Access at Siebel Center (Engineering IT knowledge base)
    https://answers.uillinois.edu/95448
    The authoritative split. Groups rooms under explicit headings:
      - "Classrooms and respective podiums":
          0216, 1103, 1105, 1109, 1111, 1131, 1214, 1302, 1304, 1404
      - "Conference Rooms and respective podiums":
          2102, 2124, 2405, 2407, 3102, 3124, 3333, 3401, 3403,
          4102, 4124, 4303, 4403, 4407
      - "Service Rooms": 2203, 3203, 3323, 4203, 4323
      - "Mail Room": 1332   "Lunch Room": 3407   "Kitchen by 2405": 2401
    NOTE: page owner Engineering IT; created 2019-10-29, last updated
    2022-10-03. Treat as possibly stale.

[2] Technology Enhanced Classrooms — Siebel room list (Technology Services)
    https://answers.illinois.edu/57175
    Lists only 1302, 1304, 1404 as TS-supported AV classrooms; says other
    classrooms are supported by Engineering IT. (None of these three are in
    our room set, but it confirms the "classroom" character of the 1xxx wing.)

[3] Engineering IT AV room directory (per-room pages)
    Siebel 0218: https://engrit.illinois.edu/services/engrit-supported-av/directory-of-av-rooms/215
        -> "Classroom - flexible seating", capacity 36.
    Siebel 0220: https://engrit.illinois.edu/services/engrit-supported-av/directory-of-av-rooms/320
        -> flexible-seating classroom, capacity 38, lecture capture + video conf.

[4] CBTF (Computer-Based Testing Facility) — Siebel lab
    https://cbtf.illinois.edu/students/labs/siebel
    Room 0403 is the CBTF testing lab on the lower level. Classified as Class
    (instructional/testing lab) rather than a meeting room.

[5] Siebel School office locations
    https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/about/office-locations
    Admin office is room 2232 (not in our room set), so no room in allrooms.txt
    is classified Admin.

PER-ROOM NOTES / CONFIDENCE
---------------------------
CONFIRMED CONFERENCE (source [1]):
  2102, 2124, 2405, 2407, 3102, 3124, 3401, 3403, 4102, 4124, 4403, 4407
  2405 also: ~70 capacity, fixed two-seat desks, video conferencing, PC.

CONFIRMED CLASS:
  0216, 1103, 1105, 1109, 1111, 1131, 1214  (source [1])
  0218 (cap 36), 0220 (cap 38)              (source [3])
  0403 CBTF testing lab                      (source [4])

LIKELY CLASS (not individually confirmed):
  0222, 0224 — same basement classroom row as 0216/0218/0220; a CS course page
  noted office hours temporarily moved to 0218/0220/0222. Left as Class.

UNKNOWN (have bookable mailboxes but no authoritative type):
  1218          — adjacent to classroom 1214; no source.
  1300 Atrium   — building atrium / common & event space, not a meeting room.
  2400D         — mailbox name says "lounge"; common space.
  2103, 2111, 2244, 2303, 4240, 4325 — sit in the upper-floor (2-4) research /
      faculty wings and are plausibly research or meeting rooms, but NO source
      confirms a type, so kept Unknown rather than guess "Research".

ADMIN / RESEARCH:
  No room in allrooms.txt is confirmed Admin (admin office 2232 is out of set)
  or confirmed Research. The upper-floor Unknowns are the Research candidates.

HOW TO UPDATE
-------------
Edit the Type field (3rd column) in allrooms.txt. The web app and the
findroom.py CLI both read types from that file directly.
